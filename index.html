Sidereal Enochian Chart

An accurate sidereal sky from NASA JPL astronomy, read through John Dee's Heptarchia, with a calendar of the Enochian workings.

Opening the Sidereal Enochian Chart ...
Open the Sidereal Enochian Chart
To put the Enochian Chart on this iPhone's home screen: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, at the top right on iPad or the bottom on iPhone), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

If it has been asleep it shows "This app has gone to sleep": tap the button there and wait about a minute.

Put it on your home screen

The page must be opened in Safari on iPhone/iPad, or in Chrome on Android.

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge): tap the three dots, then Add to home screen or Install app.

Then open it from the icon Enochian Chart (a glowing seven-pointed star over the aurora): it goes straight to the app.

If you use Use my location in the app's profile, allow location for the site. It is needed for your sunrise, which starts the Heptarchic day.

Free, no ads, no account. Sun, Moon and planets from NASA JPL (DE440s ephemeris), IAU constellation boundaries. Your position is used only to calculate and is not stored.
